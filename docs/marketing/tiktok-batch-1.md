## #1 — The Contrarian Truth  *(Myth-bust / pattern-interrupt — talking head)*
**Why:** negativity bias + pattern interrupt = highest-converting cold hook. Disarming the
hype builds trust for a "clarity" offer.

**Hooks (film 3, keep the best 3-sec retention):**
- "AI automation will **not** make you rich — at least not how you're told"
- "Stop trying to learn AI automation. Do *this* first."
- "Everyone's selling AI automation. Nobody's telling you what it actually costs."

**Script (~30s):**
- **0–3s** Hook, straight to camera, hard jump-cut.
- **3–8s** Re-hook: "Most people burn ₦500k on courses and tool subscriptions *before* they even know if it fit for them."
- **8–20s** Value: "The truth is, you can find out if AI Automation fits your skills, your time, and your budget — in **2 hours, for free.** The real cost, skills and the opportunities in Nigeria or whereever you're right now."
- **20–27s** Offer: "Saturday I'm running a free 2-hour live bootcamp — **TAAB.** 5 fast sessions, and you keep 3 clarity tools you use live."
- **27–30s** CTA: "Free. Live. This Saturday. Link in bio — spots are limited."

**On-screen text:** `THE HONEST VERSION 👇` → `cost` `skills` `who actually pays` → `Free live bootcamp — Sat`
**Caption:** Nobody talks about the *cost* of AI automation before you start. So I'm doing a free live session on it. Sat only 👇 ajbuildai.com/taab
**Hashtags:** #AIautomation #naijatech #techtok #sidehustlenigeria #n8n
**Metric to watch:** 3-sec hook rate.

## #3 — Show, Don't Tell  *(Demo / proof — screen-record or green-screen)*
**Why:** a real thing *working* on screen creates a curiosity gap nothing else can. Usually the best saves + shares driver.

**Hook:** "This little robot captures customer leads on WhatsApp while I sleep. Here's what's behind it 👇"

**Script (~30s):**
- **0–4s** Hook over a screen-grab of a message coming in → auto-reply.
- **4–18s** Cut to the n8n canvas: "No code. Drag-and-drop blocks. A message comes in → it qualifies the lead → logs it → replies." (point at nodes)
- **18–26s** Honesty beat: "It's not magic, and it's not free money. But it *is* a real skill Nigerian businesses pay for."
- **26–30s** CTA: "I break down exactly how — and whether you can do it — free, live, Saturday. Link in bio."

**On-screen text:** `no code. drag + drop.` → `lead captured ✅` → `learn it live, free — Sat`
**Caption:** Built this with zero code. Showing how it works (and if it's for you) live this Saturday — free. ajbuildai.com/taab
**Hashtags:** #n8n #automation #AIautomation #buildinpublic #naijatech
**Metric to watch:** saves + shares.

**Why:** value-first listicles get watched twice (to catch the list) and earn the "comment for the link" follow. Giving away one answer proves the room is worth it.

## How we iterate
1. **Hooks are ~80% of it.** Film 3 hook variants per video; post the strongest, keep the same body.
2. **One format per post, 1–2/day** through Friday (registration closes Fri 26th).
3. **Read the right metric per format**, not just likes. Signal ladder: **3-sec hook rate → watch-through → saves/shares → profile + link clicks.**
4. **Double down fast:** the winning *format or angle* becomes 3 variations in Batch 2; cut what flops.
5. **Keep it native** — phone-shot, fast cuts, captions burned in, trending-but-subtle audio. Ad-polish kills reach.
